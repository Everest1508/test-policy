Employee Policy Acceptance

Please review and acknowledge all company policies

1. General Policies

All employees are expected to adhere to the highest standards of professional conduct. This includes:

  • Treating all colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and dignity
  • Maintaining confidentiality of company information
  • Avoiding conflicts of interest
  • Complying with all applicable laws and regulations

Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

2. Working Hours

Our standard work hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.

Employees are expected to:

  • Arrive on time for all scheduled shifts
  • Notify their manager as soon as possible if late or absent
  • Record time accurately in the company timekeeping system
  • Request time off at least two weeks in advance when possible

Confidentiality Policy

Protecting company and customer information

1. Data Protection

As an employee, you may have access to sensitive company and customer data. You are required to:

  • Protect all login credentials and not share them with others
  • Only access data needed for your job responsibilities
  • Follow all data retention and disposal policies
  • Report any suspected data breaches immediately

This policy complies with GDPR and other applicable data protection regulations.

2. Non-Disclosure

Employees are obligated to maintain the confidentiality of:

  • Trade secrets and proprietary information
  • Customer and vendor data
  • Software implementations and technical details
  • Any other sensitive company information

This obligation continues even after employment ends.

Asset Management Policy

Proper use and care of company-provided equipment

1. Company Assets

The company may provide essential equipment including:

  • Laptop and laptop bag
  • Mobile device and SIM card
  • ID cards and access badges
  • Other necessary equipment

Employees are responsible for the proper use and care of all assigned assets.

2. Loss or Damage

In case of loss or damage to company assets:

  • Mobile Phones: Report immediately to HR. Employee responsible for ₹10,000 for lost device.
  • Laptops: Report to IT and HR. Employee responsible for ₹20,000 for lost device.
  • ID Cards: ₹300 replacement fee for lost cards.

Costs will be deducted from salary or must be paid within 30 days.
